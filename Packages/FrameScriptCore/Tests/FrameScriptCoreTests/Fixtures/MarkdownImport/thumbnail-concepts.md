# Conceptos de miniatura — Velocity Ridge 3.0 and Sandbox Valley 0.60

Goal: each proposal should sell a different idea even without the title. Recommended text limit: 2–3 words.

---

# Velocity Ridge 3.0

Título de referencia: **VELOCITY RIDGE 3.0: DOES IT FINALLY MAKE SENSE?**

## Concepto A — FINALLY? — Recomendado

**Idea:** relief and a return, with one doubt left open.

- Large night car in the foreground, leaving a dark area for a lit stage.
- Timing board and repair bay blurred in the background but still recognisable.
- Small green `3.0` badge.
- Text: **FINALLY?**
- Palette: grey and black for the past; green and orange for the new area.

**Por qué funciona:** it completes the question in the title without repeating it and captures the argument: there is structure now, but the verdict is not fully positive.

## Concepto B — TOO LATE?

**Idea:** a good patch weighed down by the roadmap.

- Diagonal split.
- Right side: car, timing board and repair bay, clean and bright.
- Left side: an old calendar and a locked online icon.
- Text: **TOO LATE?**
- Palette: green and blue for 3.0; muted red for the promises.

**Por qué funciona:** it sells conflict and opinion. It is the stronger option if the chosen title mentions the delay.

## Concepto C — ALIVE AGAIN?

**Idea:** career mode moves from empty to competitive.

- Empty garage on the left with dark silhouettes.
- A full team panel and two cars entering the workshop on the right.
- A pulse line or rising arrow crossing the frame.
- Text: **ALIVE AGAIN?**
- No face; a more editorial composition.

**Por qué funciona:** it visualises the problem the patch tries to solve and depends less on the new content.

## Prueba A/B

1. **A — FINALLY?**: curiosity and return; main option.
2. **B — TOO LATE?**: conflict and opinion; contrast option.

Avoid: putting the car, the board, the bay, the roadmap, a face and five text blocks in one image. The thumbnail should sell one tension.

# Sandbox Valley 0.60

Título de referencia: **SANDBOX VALLEY 0.60: THE BIGGEST CHANGE… AND THE MOST CONTROVERSIAL**

## Concepto A — DIFFERENT GAME — Recomendado

**Idea:** a generational jump visible at a glance.

- Vertical comparison of the same car and camera angle.
- Left: old look, flat light and a small `BEFORE` label.
- Right: new night lighting, volumetric clouds and headlights; label `AFTER`.
- The new car crossing the split to connect both halves.
- Text: **DIFFERENT GAME**

**Por qué funciona:** it shows the largest change without assuming an expert viewer. The before/after reads in under a second.

## Concepto B — AT WHAT COST?

**Idea:** spectacular visuals against a performance loss.

- Attractive night scene filling the whole thumbnail.
- Large counter: `80 FPS → 42 FPS`, with the second number in red.
- Car slightly blurred behind the counter.
- Text: **AT WHAT COST?**

**Por qué funciona:** it frames the most concrete community argument and attracts both admirers and worried owners.

## Concepto C — TOO MUCH

**Idea:** the studio is rebuilding too many systems in one patch.

- Car in the centre.
- Only three tilted plates around it: `VISUALS`, `MENUS`, `PHYSICS`.
- A crack or warning sign crossing the plates.
- Text: **TOO MUCH**
- Orange palette with black and red.

**Por qué funciona:** it sells the full editorial thesis: huge ambition and a difficult landing. Less technical than the frame-rate concept.

## Prueba A/B

1. **A — DIFFERENT GAME**: visual transformation; main option.
2. **B — AT WHAT COST?**: performance argument; contrast option.

Avoid: a pretty night screenshot with no comparison, text or conflict. It can look like promotional art and gives no reason to click.

# Jerarquía visual común

1. One large protagonist: car, counter or comparison.
2. One visual contradiction.
3. Two or three words, readable on a phone.
4. Small game logo; do not compete with the message.
5. Leave one clean corner so the duration badge does not cover information.
