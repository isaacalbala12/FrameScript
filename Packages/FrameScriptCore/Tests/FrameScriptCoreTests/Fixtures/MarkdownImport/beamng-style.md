# Guion

## 0:00 — Hook

[IMAGEN: quick comparison between the old build and the new one, daytime and night]

Sandbox Valley just shipped its biggest patch ever. New lighting, a new menu system, a new car, airflow between vehicles and more than forty achievements.

The community reaction is perfect for this game: some players gained twenty frames, while others with expensive hardware lost almost half.

So the question is not whether 0.60 changes Sandbox Valley, because it changes everything. The question is whether the change is finished.

## 0:55 — Nine years in one patch

[IMAGEN: a version timeline and the patch menu]

Sandbox Valley has been in early access for nine years. Patch 0.60 does not just add a map: it rebuilds rendering, lighting, menus, controls and part of the vehicle simulation.

The main build arrived on 2 April. Then came 0.60.1, 0.60.2 and 0.60.3. That pace tells two stories: the team reacts quickly, but the launch needed stabilisation.

## 1:50 — The visual jump

[COMPARATIVA: same car, map, time of day and camera]

Shadows have been rebuilt so they no longer float. The patch also adds physical lighting, auto exposure and a sky with volumetric clouds.

The night change is the clearest: headlights, reflections and fog are much easier to read. It is not just prettier; it changes how you see the road.

The visual jump has a cost, though. The studio itself warns that image quality rises much more than in previous patches.

## 3:30 — The new menus

[IMAGEN: pause menu with a controller and with a mouse]

The pause menu has been redesigned from scratch. You can manage traffic, edit the HUD, control replays and change graphics options with a preview.

With a controller it is clearly better. With a keyboard and mouse there are more layers and fewer direct shortcuts than before.

[PRUEBA: change car, part and setup with a controller, then repeat with a mouse]

The fair test is not whether it looks modern. It is timing real tasks: loading a setup, swapping a part and managing a replay.

## 5:00 — Airflow between vehicles

[IMAGEN: two cars on a straight with distance data]

The physics feature I care about most is that vehicles no longer behave as if they were alone in the world.

You now get slipstream, side draft and bump draft. A truck can move nearby objects without touching them.

[PRUEBA PROPUESTA: top speed alone versus following another car, with identical distance and conditions]

The point is to prove it with numbers instead of reading the change list out loud.

## 6:15 — New car, maps and photo mode

[IMAGEN: the new car and its body variants]

The new car is the Marrow Tern, a light sports model with petrol and electric versions. It has enough mechanical variety to avoid feeling like one body with different paint.

[IMAGEN: the new map and pace notes]

There is also a mountain map with three note levels, virtual rivals and a tutorial for newcomers.

Photo mode has been rebuilt with presets, a gallery and motion blur. It does not change driving, but for creators it is a huge improvement.

## 7:50 — Performance: the big fight

[GRÁFICA: frames, one-percent lows, RAM and VRAM across three renderers]

Now the difficult part. The studio announced memory optimisations and many players heard “more frames”. Those are not the same thing.

If you had memory to spare, saving memory does not create frames. If you were at the limit, it can reduce stutter and allow more traffic.

[PRUEBA PROPUESTA]

To check your own machine: test without mods, compare renderers, lower shadows before the whole preset and look at one-percent lows as well as the average.

[RE-HOOK]

And part of this transition exists because the studio is preparing something that seemed impossible a few years ago.

## 9:45 — Consoles, online and tyres

[IMAGEN: a console announcement and an official statement]

Sandbox Valley will come to consoles later this year. There is no exact date, and the team says it will not promise one until it is sure.

Official online play also exists, but it is far away. When it arrives it will be a first implementation, not a finished package.

The new tyre model is in development, but it is not part of 0.60. Anyone who says otherwise is mixing the present with the roadmap.

## 11:10 — Verdict

[IMAGEN: the new car at night and a final crash]

Sandbox Valley 0.60 is fascinating because it shows the future and, at the same time, every problem involved in reaching it.

It is the most spectacular version of the game so far. Lighting, sky, the new car and airflow make the sandbox deeper.

But if your machine is modest, you play at high resolution or you rely on many mods, do not assume everything will improve.

Patch 0.60 is the price of modernising a nine-year-old game without erasing what makes it unique. Now the studio has to prove it can polish the foundation.

Tell me in the comments what you would prioritise: frame rate, tyres or online play. See you in the next video.
