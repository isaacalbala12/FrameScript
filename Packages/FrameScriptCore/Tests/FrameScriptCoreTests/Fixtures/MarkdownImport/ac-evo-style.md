# Guion

## 0:00 — Hook

[IMAGEN: fast cut of night stages, a repair bay and a timing board]

Velocity Ridge 3.0 is out, and on paper it is the update many players requested: a career structure, damage with consequences and an editor for building custom routes.

There is a catch, though. The content promised for launch is smaller than expected, and the new menu layout has split the community.

So today we separate the changes that matter from the ones that only sound good in a patch note.

## 0:40 — The 2.5 problem

[IMAGEN: open map with route icons and one parked car]

Driving was never the issue. The stages felt good, the engine audio worked and the car had real weight on loose surfaces.

The issue was that after three events there was no clear reason to keep going. Credits bought nothing interesting and the garage grew without consequence.

Version 3.0 tries to answer exactly that question: what do I do once I already know how to drive?

## 1:40 — Career mode

[CAPTURA: team screen, budget panel and season calendar]

Career is now split into seasons. You start with a small team, pick events and divide a fixed budget between the car, repairs and travel.

The important change is that money no longer buys the fastest machine. It buys the ability to finish a season without breaking the team.

## 3:10 — Damage and repairs

[PRUEBA EN PANTALLA: a heavy impact during a stage and the repair screen]

This is the part I find most interesting. Damage no longer disappears between stages: every impact has a cost and a workshop delay.

[RE-HOOK]

That turns driving quickly without wrecking the car into an actual decision instead of a marketing line.

## 4:35 — Gravel tyres

[B-ROLL: slow motion of a wheel climbing out of a rut]

The new tyre model is most obvious when the surface gets dirty. Grip disappears earlier, but the slide is easier to read.

[NOTA DE GRABACIÓN: compare a fresh set with a worn set on the same stage after testing three cars]

It is not a revolution, but it changes how you plan a long run.

## 6:15 — Stages and editor

[IMAGEN: the three new stages and the route editor]

Version 3.0 adds three stages: one fast gravel loop, one technical forest section and one night route. The night route is the strongest of the three and also the most demanding.

The route editor lets you place checkpoints and share short codes. It is simple, it works, and it has already produced some very strange ideas.

## 7:30 — Menus and performance

[IMAGEN: the new menus and an on-screen frame counter]

The new menu layout is clearer on a controller and slower with a keyboard and mouse. It is not a disaster, but you have to relearn where everything lives.

On performance, the first patch fixes the worst drops. Even so, test it yourself before assuming that every machine benefits.

[RE-HOOK]

And that leaves the question that shapes the next year: what happens with online racing and consoles?

## 9:25 — Roadmap and community

[IMAGEN: an official statement and a wall of community replies]

The studio confirms online racing for a later date, with no month attached. Consoles are still planned, but the team refuses to promise a window.

Players are split between those who value the new structure and those who think the content arrived too late. Both views can be true.

## 10:35 — Verdict

[IMAGEN: a car crossing the finish line at night]

Velocity Ridge 3.0 is the update the game needed, though not the one everyone expected.

If you left during 2.5, there is now a real reason to return. If you wanted a complete online mode, you will have to wait. And if the price already felt high, this release does not turn it into an impulse purchase.

Tell me in the comments what the next update should prioritize: more stages, online racing or a deeper editor. See you in the next video.
