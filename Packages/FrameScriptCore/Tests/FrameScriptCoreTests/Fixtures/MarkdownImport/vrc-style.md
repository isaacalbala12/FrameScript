## 1:30 — Editions and installation

[CAPTURA: installation folder and content manager]

There are two editions. The basic one works without mods: install and drive. The advanced one needs a content manager and a recent shader package, which is paid.

To install it, drag the folders by hand and create a control profile just for this car. You will need it, because the wheel has more buttons than a cockpit should.

## 2:15 — The hybrid system in 75 seconds

[IMAGEN: manual page with figures and a power-versus-speed graph]

Figures from the manual: a 1.6-litre V6, 15,000 rpm, about 1,000 horsepower and a 350-kilowatt electric motor.

First, the key idea: energy is not power. Energy is what fits in the battery; power is how quickly you spend it.

Second, the 350 kilowatts do not last for the whole straight. Above roughly 290 km/h the electric output starts to fall.

[RE-HOOK]

And the car that slows with the throttle pinned? That is straight-line clipping: the electric motor recharges while you accelerate and steals speed.

## 3:30 — The wheel display

[GRABAR: camera close to the wheel; arrows point at each element]

Green bar on the left: battery charge. Horizontal bar at the top: instant power. Purple bar on the right: energy recovered during the lap.

The first three LEDs belong to the active wing. White: you can press. Blue: pre-armed. Off: wing open. Amber: you are late.

## 4:30 — Three buttons that are not the same

[ROTULO: three cards labelled WING / BOOST / OVERTAKE]

This confuses almost everyone.

**Active wing**: opens the front and rear flaps on a straight. Less drag and less energy use, but no extra energy.

**Boost**: a free button. You spend battery now, whenever you decide.

**Overtake**: replaces the classic passing aid. Cross the detection line close to the car ahead and you get more power for that lap.

## 5:10 — Energy maps and adaptation

[CAPTURA: deployment map menu with twenty-four sectors and a qualifying-versus-race graph]

This is where the mod becomes wild. There are twelve deployment maps, each split into as many as twenty-four track sectors.

In every sector you choose where deployment starts, where it stops and where clipping begins.

[IMAGEN: qualifying map compared with the race map]

In qualifying you start near 90 percent and finish at zero. In a race you start at 45 and finish at 45.

## 6:20 — Zone editor and the hidden rule

[CAPTURA: zone editor in the side bar]

Because the car depends on zones, the studio built an editor for placing them on any track, including mod tracks.

There is also a rule almost nobody knows: under full throttle, electric output can only be cut by 150 kilowatts at once. The car must give you at least 200 kilowatts for one second.

[IMAGEN: telemetry showing a 200 kW step for one second]

## 7:00 — Car setup

[CAPTURA: integrated setup menu, tab by tab]

The brakes are brake-by-wire with migration. You brake hard at the front and, as you release, the balance moves rearward to avoid locking.

The differential locks differently on entry, mid-corner and exit. Closed louvers add downforce; open ones add cooling. Watch temperature, because the motor heats up in traffic.

## 7:50 — How it actually drives

[GRABAR: a lap on a technical circuit with telemetry beside the wheel]

First: there is no grip in first or second. It feels like ice. From third upward the downforce works and the car becomes a delight.

Second, braking: it is no longer a long zone, it is a moment. Do not stab the pedal; squeeze it.

Third: do not combine braking and steering. Brake straight, release, let the car roll and open the throttle very gently.

Is all this complexity worth it against the alternatives? Let us compare.

## 8:50 — Comparison

[TABLA EN PANTALLA: the four columns in the table below]

**Against last year’s model.** The 2027 car already had electronic brakes and a moving rear wing. The 2028 car swaps that for active flaps and adds configurable clipping.

**Against the rival simulator.** The rival has a licence and better audio, but its hybrid system is more restricted and its zones cannot be edited.

[ROTULO: “Community impressions, not confirmed at every circuit”]

**Against the big publisher.** The publisher sells a season pack, not a new game. It has a licence and a real grid, but less adjustment.

## 10:00 — Problems and verdict

[IMAGEN: a race against the AI, the player passing with an empty battery]

Now the problems. The AI cannot manage energy: you can overtake it with a flat battery. The advanced edition also pushes you into the paid shader package.

If you want to understand the 2028 season, nothing else comes close. If you just want a Sunday race, use the basic edition or the official game.
